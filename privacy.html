[ English Version ]

Jahhat Media (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users, subscribers, and community members in accordance with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, process, share, and protect your personal data when you use our website (jahhat.com), subscribe to our email newsletter, or interact with our services.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us when subscribing to our newsletter, contacting us, or filling out forms on our website. Newsletter sign-ups made through the “Subscribe to Dispatch” form are collected and stored using Brevo, our email marketing platform (see Section 3):

  • Full Name (Obligatory for newsletter subscription)
  • Email Address (Obligatory for delivery of the newsletter and our communications)
  • Communication Preferences & Consent Logs (Records of your opt-in choices, including whether you agreed to receive our newsletter and, optionally, to the sharing of your details with marketing partners, together with the date and time of your sign-up)
  • Technical Data (IP addresses, browser type, and basic traffic logs collected via standard web servers)

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your personal data is collected and processed for the following commercial and operational purposes:

  • Delivering “The Weekly Dispatch” newsletter, local scene news, event listings, video premieres, and merchandise announcements, and managing our newsletter subscriber list through Brevo.
  • Sending marketing and promotional emails from Jahhat Media to subscribers who have agreed to receive them.
  • Responding to user inquiries, contact form messages, or media submissions.
  • Sending relevant promotional offers, gear drops, and industry updates from authorized brand partners and sponsors (where explicit opt-in consent is provided).
  • Fulfilling statutory obligations and protecting our legal rights under Malaysian law.

3. Consent and Third-Party Disclosure

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent. Your data may be disclosed to the following classes of third parties:

  • Brevo (email marketing platform): Your name, email address and consent choices are collected and stored by Brevo, which sends our newsletter and marketing emails on our behalf. Brevo processes this data under our instructions, and we remain responsible for how it is used for these purposes. Brevo may also use some data on its own side to keep its platform secure (for example, to detect attempted fraud or hacking). For details, see Brevo’s Privacy Policy.
  • Web Hosting Providers: Hosting infrastructure strictly for website functionality.
  • Marketing Partners & Sponsors: Selected brand partners, local skateshops, and event sponsors within the action sports industry (only if you explicitly checked the partner consent box).
  • We Never Sell Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.

Transfer outside Malaysia: Brevo is an international service, so your name and email address may be stored and processed on servers located outside Malaysia. By submitting the sign-up form, you consent to this transfer for the purposes described in this notice.

3.1 Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosure

We may display advertising on jahhat.com through third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense. This section explains how cookies are used for advertising.

  • Third-Party Vendors & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to jahhat.com or other websites.
  • DART / Advertising Cookies: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Your Opt-Out Rights: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or through AboutAds (https://www.aboutads.info/choices).

4. Your Rights Under the Malaysian PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access & Correct: Request access to or correction of your personal data held by us.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent / Opt-Out: Withdraw consent or limit the processing of your data for direct marketing at any time.
  • Right to Data Deletion: Request the complete removal of your personal details from our distribution lists.

5. How to Opt-Out or Request Data Deletion

To exercise your rights, update your preferences, or request complete data deletion:

  • Email Us: Send an email to privacy@jahhat.com or contact@jahhat.com with the subject line “PDPA Data Request / Opt-Out”.
  • Direct Unsubscribe: Click the “Unsubscribe” link provided at the footer of every newsletter email sent by Jahhat Media. This removes you from our Brevo mailing list.

We will process and fulfill your request within 14 working days of receipt. Where you ask us to delete your data, we will also remove it from Brevo.

6. Security & Data Retention

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from loss, misuse, or unauthorized access. We retain newsletter subscriber data for as long as you remain subscribed, and delete it when you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, unless we are required by law to keep it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Notice

Jahhat Media reserves the right to update this Privacy Notice periodically to reflect legal requirements or operational changes. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated “Effective Date”.

[ Versi Bahasa Malaysia ]

Jahhat Media (“kami”) menghormati privasi anda dan komited untuk melindungi data peribadi pengguna, pelanggan, dan ahli komuniti kami selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (APDP).

Notis Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana kami mengumpul, mengguna, memproses, menzahirkan, dan melindungi data peribadi anda apabila anda menggunakan laman web kami (jahhat.com), melanggan buletin emel kami, atau berinteraksi dengan perkhidmatan kami.

1. Data Peribadi Yang Dikumpul

Kami mengumpul data peribadi yang anda berikan secara sukarela apabila melanggan buletin kami atau mengisi borang di laman web kami. Pendaftaran buletin melalui borang “Subscribe to Dispatch” dikumpul dan disimpan menggunakan Brevo, platform pemasaran emel kami (lihat Bahagian 3):

  • Nama Penuh (Wajib untuk tujuan langganan buletin)
  • Alamat Emel (Wajib untuk penghantaran buletin dan komunikasi kami)
  • Log Keizinan & Pilihan Komunikasi (Rekod pilihan opt-in anda, termasuk persetujuan anda untuk menerima buletin kami dan, secara pilihan, perkongsian maklumat anda dengan rakan pemasaran, bersama tarikh dan masa pendaftaran)
  • Data Teknikal (Alamat IP, jenis pelayar, dan log trafik asas)

2. Tujuan Pengumpulan Data

Data peribadi anda diproses untuk tujuan komersial dan operasi berikut:

  • Menghantar buletin emel “The Weekly Dispatch”, berita tempatan, senarai acara, tayangan perdana video, dan pengumuman barangan, serta mengurus senarai pelanggan buletin kami melalui Brevo.
  • Menghantar emel pemasaran dan promosi daripada Jahhat Media kepada pelanggan yang bersetuju menerimanya.
  • Membalas pertanyaan, borang mesej, atau penyerahan media anda.
  • Menghantar tawaran promosi daripada rakan jenama dan penaja yang diizinkan (sekiranya keizinan eksplisit diberikan).
  • Mematuhi kewajipan undang-undang di bawah undang-undang Malaysia.

3. Keizinan dan Pendedahan Pihak Ketiga

Data peribadi anda diproses berdasarkan keizinan eksplisit anda dan mungkin didedahkan kepada kelas pihak ketiga berikut:

  • Brevo (platform pemasaran emel): Nama, alamat emel dan pilihan keizinan anda dikumpul dan disimpan oleh Brevo, yang menghantar buletin dan emel pemasaran kami bagi pihak kami. Brevo memproses data ini mengikut arahan kami, dan kami kekal bertanggungjawab atas penggunaannya untuk tujuan ini. Brevo juga boleh menggunakan sebahagian data di pihaknya untuk menjaga keselamatan platform (contohnya, mengesan cubaan penipuan atau penggodaman). Untuk maklumat lanjut, sila lihat Dasar Privasi Brevo.
  • Penyedia Hos Laman Web: Infrastruktur hos semata-mata untuk fungsi laman web.
  • Rakan Pemasaran & Penaja: Jenama sukan aksi, kedai papan seluncur tempatan, dan penaja acara (hanya jika anda menanda kotak keizinan penaja).
  • Kami Tidak Menjual Data: Kami tidak menjual atau menyewakan data peribadi anda kepada mana-mana pihak ketiga.

Pemindahan ke luar Malaysia: Brevo ialah perkhidmatan antarabangsa, maka nama dan alamat emel anda mungkin disimpan dan diproses di pelayan yang terletak di luar Malaysia. Dengan menghantar borang pendaftaran, anda bersetuju dengan pemindahan ini untuk tujuan yang dinyatakan dalam notis ini.

3.1 Pendedahan Pengiklanan Pihak Ketiga & Google AdSense

Kami mungkin memaparkan iklan di jahhat.com melalui perkhidmatan pengiklanan pihak ketiga, termasuk Google AdSense. Bahagian ini menjelaskan bagaimana kuki digunakan untuk pengiklanan.

  • Penjual Pihak Ketiga & Kuki: Penjual pihak ketiga, termasuk Google, menggunakan kuki untuk menyiarkan iklan berdasarkan kunjungan terdahulu anda ke jahhat.com atau laman web lain.
  • Kuki Pengiklanan: Penggunaan kuki pengiklanan oleh Google membolehkannya dan rakan kongsinya menyiarkan iklan kepada anda berdasarkan lawatan anda ke laman web ini dan/atau laman web lain di Internet.
  • Hak Penolakan (Opt-Out) Anda: Anda boleh menarik balik keizinan pengiklanan peribadi dengan melawati Tetapan Iklan Google (https://www.google.com/settings/ads) atau melalui AboutAds (https://www.aboutads.info/choices).

4. Hak Anda di Bawah APDP Malaysia

Di bawah Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010, anda berhak untuk:

  • Meminta akses dan pembetulan data peribadi anda yang disimpan oleh kami.
  • Menarik balik keizinan atau mengehadkan pemprosesan data anda untuk tujuan pemasaran langsung pada bila-bila masa.
  • Meminta pemadaman lengkap data peribadi anda daripada senarai edaran kami.

5. Cara Menarik Balik Keizinan / Pemadaman Data

Untuk mengehadkan pemprosesan atau memadamkan data peribadi anda:

  • Emel Kami: Hantar emel ke privacy@jahhat.com atau contact@jahhat.com dengan subjek “Permohonan Data APDP / Opt-Out”.
  • Pautan Nyahlanggan: Klik pautan “Unsubscribe” di bahagian bawah setiap emel buletin kami. Ini akan mengeluarkan anda daripada senarai mel Brevo kami.

Permohonan anda akan diproses dalam tempoh 14 hari kerja. Jika anda meminta data anda dipadam, kami juga akan memadamkannya daripada Brevo.

6. Keselamatan dan Penyimpanan Data

Kami melaksanakan langkah-langkah keselamatan teknikal dan organisasi untuk melindungi data peribadi anda daripada kehilangan, penyalahgunaan, atau akses yang tidak dibenarkan. Data pelanggan buletin disimpan selagi anda kekal melanggan, dan dipadam apabila anda berhenti melanggan atau meminta pemadaman, melainkan kami dikehendaki oleh undang-undang untuk menyimpannya.

7. Perubahan Kepada Notis Privasi Ini

Jahhat Media berhak mengemas kini Notis Privasi ini dari semasa ke semasa bagi mencerminkan keperluan undang-undang atau perubahan operasi. Sebarang kemas kini akan diterbitkan terus di halaman ini bersama “Tarikh Kuat Kuasa” yang dikemas kini.
