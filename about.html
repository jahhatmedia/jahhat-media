Est. Malaysia

This Is JAHHAT MEDIA

Not a magazine. Not a brand deck. A record of what's actually happening on Malaysia's concrete - built by the people rolling on it.

Started with one camera and no plan.

JAHHAT MEDIA started out of curiosity and necessity. The mainstream didn't care about what was actually happening on the ground, and for those of us living it, there was no central hub, just scattered group chats, dead forums, and outdated information. After talking to other local skaters and realizing everyone was facing the exact same void, we decided to build the JAHHAT MEDIA ourselves.

What began as raw documentation of skating scene sessions has evolved into something bigger: a single, unified source of information, a hub for Malaysia’s skate, rollerblade, and underground subculture scene. We exist to bridge the gap for our skaters, give aspiring skaters a direct line to learn and connect, and showcase the vibrant, raw energy of our scene to the rest of the world.

Today, JAHHAT MEDIA operates as a street-level wire service and a landing launchpad for foreign riders looking to hit Malaysian spots and link up with the local community. No outside narrators, no fluff, no corporate filter - just an unfiltered home for the subculture, built by people who ride, for everyone who wants in. Welcome to JAHHAT MEDIA

The Manifesto

01

Authentic Stories

Every profile, spot check and gear review is reported first-hand. If we didn't shoot it, ride it, or talk to the person directly, it doesn't run.

02

Community First

The scene owns this platform as much as we do. Submissions, tips and spot intel from skaters shape the coverage.

03

Raw Media

No over-produced gloss. We keep the grain, the real people, the real conditions - because that's what the streets actually look like.
