Get In Touch With Us

Contact & Submissions

Got raw footage, a spot worth checking, or a sponsorship inquiry? This is the direct line to the Jahhat Media crew.

Submission Guidelines

Sending raw clips or photos? Here's how to get them seen:

  • Upload footage to Google Drive or YouTube (unlisted) and drop the link in your message
  • Include the rider's name, the spot location, and when it was shot
  • Original, unedited clips are preferred - we handle the edit
  • Spot submissions should note ground condition and any security presence and any other relevant information
